Analytical Sample Case Study 01 Artifact

Regulatory Harmonization Matrix — RPerMen Inovasi Pelayanan Publik

A structured four-dimension review framework applied during regulatory drafting to eliminate normative ambiguity and maintain auditability across multi-stakeholder revisions:

Analytical Dimension Review Focus & Criteria Illustrative Output / Action Taken
1. Terminology Consistency Cross-checking definitions in General Provisions (Ketentuan Umum) against operational articles and higher-level laws. Standardized recurring institutional terms and innovation classifications to prevent conflicting interpretations across ministries.
2. Norm Clarity Evaluating whether mandates, authorities, and procedural timelines are actionable without excessive administrative discretion. Flagged vague obligations and proposed concise, enforceable phrasing aligned with Indonesian legislative drafting standards (UU 12/2011).
3. Change Traceability Maintaining side-by-side version control (Matriks Sandingan) between legacy provisions, draft revisions, and stakeholder inputs. Logged article-by-article rationales for every addition, deletion, or restructuring to support leadership briefings.
4. Open Issues Isolating unresolved cross-unit policy debates, legal-authority boundaries, or technical implementation bottlenecks. Compiled prioritized issue-tracking briefs with decision options for Assistant Deputy and inter-ministerial harmonization meetings.
